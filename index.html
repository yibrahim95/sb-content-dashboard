## Q3 performance summary


**Distribution, not content, is the Smash Balloon story.** SB's YouTube reach collapsed in early July when its Shorts stopped being served into the Shorts feed. Studio puts Q3 SB views at 1,268. The same scripts posted to TikTok performed far better over the same weeks, so this is a channel/distribution fault, not a content-quality one — and it's recovering, not fixed.


**WPChat is flat on the metric that matters.** 12 of 30 subscribers, zero gained in Q3, everything published as shorts. The channel's own history is clear: long-form setup/tutorial guides convert roughly 19× better than shorts (0.38 vs 0.02 subs per 100 views) and pull 4× the CTR. The rest of the quarter hinges on shipping searchable long-form, not more shorts.


**Output is behind but within reach.** ~11.8 videos/week against the 14/week target (1 post/day per brand). Close, and recoverable with a steady daily cadence rather than spiky weeks.


**The two KPIs pull against each other.** Retention sits at 37.5% vs a 60% goal, but long-form (the subscriber lever) retains less than shorts — so 60% retention and 30 subscribers cannot both be optimised. Needs an explicit call.


**Where it's won:** the video quality audit (Rock 1) is effectively done — 17 fixes shipped with before/after evidence, QC gates now running on every video. And the iMessage skill was adopted outside the video team for the WPVibe launch, closing the external-adoption goal.


**Behind and needs a sprint:** TikTok experiments (1 of 10) and comments left on other accounts (3 of 30).
